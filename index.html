About

Field notes

I’m a robotics graduate student advised by Prof. Xiaolong Wang at UC San Diego, with dual bachelor’s degrees in Materials Science and Computer Science from Tsinghua University. My work sits at the seam of embodiment and intelligence: optimizing robot hardware for the real world, shipping LLM backends that survive long-context failure modes, and turning early product ideas into usable systems.

Lately that means contributing to Transnode AI’s AuthenlyUSA verification product, hardening the Agent Arena simulation stack, and prototyping low-cost dexterous hands and teleoperation platforms in the lab.
